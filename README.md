## About
The National Coordination Point Research Data Management (LCRDM) is a national **network** of experts in the field of research data management (RDM). The LCRDM forms the link between **policy and solution**. Within the LCRDM, experts work together to put RDM topics on the agenda that require a joint national approach. Outputs which are suitable for reuse, with maybe some adaptations for your own institute can be found here.

If you have something to share? Please send a message to info@lcrdm.nl
